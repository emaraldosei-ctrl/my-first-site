    Quality improvement roadmap

    Illustrative PDSA cycle for reducing inpatient falls

    1. Plan
      Identify the risk

      Analyse fall events by time of day, unit and Morse score to target night-shift toileting needs.

    2. Do
      Test a change

      Pilot hourly purposeful rounding and bed-exit alarms on one medical-surgical unit.

    3. Study
      Measure the impact

      Compare fall rates per 1,000 patient days on run charts against the baseline period.

    4. Act
      Adopt & scale

      Standardise the bundle, educate staff and spread to high-risk units with ongoing audits.

    About the project

    Where nursing expertise meets data and AI

    Hospital Insights is a portfolio project built to show how clinical knowledge, analytical skill and careful AI evaluation can work together to improve healthcare decisions. Nurses see the patient story behind every number — analytics makes that story visible at scale.

    Nursing expertise

    Defines which metrics truly matter at the bedside — from fall risk and pressure injuries to safe staffing — and provides the clinical context to interpret them.

    Healthcare data analytics

    Transforms operational and quality data into clear trends, benchmarks and dashboards that help leaders allocate beds and staff before pressure points become harm.

    AI evaluation

    Critically assesses AI tools for accuracy, bias, transparency and safety, ensuring predictions support — never replace — professional clinical judgement.

    Responsible AI principles

    • Human-in-the-loop review for every clinical recommendation
    • Fairness checks across patient groups and units
    • Explainable outputs that clinicians can question
    • Privacy by design — synthetic data for demonstrations
